About White Tea

White tea is a delicate type of tea made from the young leaves and unopened buds of the Camellia sinensis plant.

It undergoes very little processing, which helps preserve its light colour, mild flavour, and natural aroma.

White tea is often described as having a subtle, slightly sweet taste. It contains antioxidants and some caffeine, although the amount varies depending on the tea and how it is prepared.

It is usually brewed with hot rather than boiling water to protect its delicate flavour.

Popular Varieties

  • Silver Needle
  • White Peony
  • Long Life Eyebrow
  • Shou Mei
Brewing tip: Use water at around 75–85°C and steep for 3–5 minutes. Adjust the brewing time to suit your preferred flavour.
