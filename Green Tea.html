About Green Tea

Green tea is a popular and refreshing beverage made from the leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant.

It is known for its light taste and natural antioxidants.

Many people drink green tea as part of a healthy lifestyle because it contains compounds that may support overall health.

It can be enjoyed hot or cold and is available in many varieties. Drinking green tea in moderation can be a pleasant way to stay refreshed.

Popular Varieties

  • Japanese Sencha
  • Matcha Green Tea
  • Jasmine Green Tea
  • Gunpowder Green Tea
Brewing tip: Use water at around 70–80°C and steep for 2–3 minutes to help keep your green tea smooth and refreshing.
