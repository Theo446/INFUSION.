Discover the Art of Brewing Tea

Every cup of tea tells a story. Learn how to choose the right water temperature, steeping time and tea quantity to create the perfect cup with INFUSION.

Explore Our Brewing Guides

From delicate green teas to comforting herbal blends, follow our simple guides to bring out the best flavour in every infusion.

1. Green Tea

Light, fresh and delicate, green tea is best brewed gently to preserve its natural flavour without developing bitterness.

  • Water temperature: 70–80°C
  • Tea quantity: 1 teaspoon per cup
  • Steeping time: 2–3 minutes
  • Flavour: Fresh and grassy
Brewing tip: Avoid boiling water. Slightly cooler water helps keep green tea smooth and refreshing.

2. Black Tea

Bold, rich and full-bodied, black tea is a wonderful choice for a morning boost or a relaxing afternoon tea break.

  • Water temperature: 95–100°C
  • Tea quantity: 1 teaspoon per cup
  • Steeping time: 3–5 minutes
  • Flavour: Rich and robust
Brewing tip: Add milk, lemon or honey if you enjoy a different flavour profile. Taste before adding anything.

3. White Tea

Delicate and subtly sweet, white tea deserves a gentle brewing method that allows its light, floral character to shine.

  • Water temperature: 75–85°C
  • Tea quantity: 1–2 teaspoons per cup
  • Steeping time: 3–5 minutes
  • Flavour: Soft and floral
Brewing tip: Use fresh-tasting water and allow the tea to steep patiently for a gentle, aromatic cup.

4. Herbal Tea

Enjoy the comforting aromas of ingredients such as peppermint, chamomile, ginger and dried fruit. Herbal infusions are naturally varied in flavour.

  • Water temperature: 95–100°C
  • Tea quantity: Follow package directions
  • Steeping time: 5–7 minutes
  • Flavour: Floral, fruity or refreshing
Brewing tip: Cover your cup while steeping to help retain the infusion's delicate aromas. Follow the blend's packaging for the best results.

5. Oolong Tea

Smooth, aromatic and beautifully balanced, oolong tea sits between green and black tea. Its flavour can range from light and floral to rich and toasty, depending on how the leaves are processed.

  • Water temperature: 85–95°C
  • Tea quantity: 1–2 teaspoons per cup
  • Steeping time: 3–5 minutes
  • Flavour: Floral, fruity or toasty
Brewing tip: Experiment with different steeping times to discover your preferred flavour. Oolong tea leaves can often be steeped multiple times, revealing new aromas with each infusion.

Your Perfect Cup in Four Steps

Great tea starts with a few simple habits. Follow these steps every time you brew.

1

Choose Your Tea

Select your favourite loose-leaf tea or tea bag and measure the recommended quantity.

2

Heat the Water

Use fresh water and select a temperature suited to your chosen tea.

3

Steep with Care

Set a timer and allow your tea to infuse for the recommended amount of time.

4

Sip and Enjoy

Remove the tea leaves or bag, let your tea cool slightly and savour every sip.
