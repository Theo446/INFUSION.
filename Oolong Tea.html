About Oolong Tea

Oolong tea is a traditional Chinese tea known for its partially oxidised leaves, placing it between green and black tea in flavour and colour.

This unique level of oxidation gives oolong a rich, layered taste that can range from light and floral to warm, toasty, and slightly sweet.

Different varieties offer distinctive aromas and flavours, making oolong an interesting choice for both new tea drinkers and experienced tea lovers.

Oolong tea contains caffeine, with the amount varying depending on the variety and preparation.

Popular Varieties

  • Tie Guan Yin
  • Da Hong Pao
  • Milk Oolong
  • Oriental Beauty
Brewing tip: Use water at around 85–95°C and steep for 3–5 minutes. Adjust the temperature and time depending on the variety and your taste.
