About Black Tea

Black tea is a popular beverage made from fully oxidized tea leaves, giving it a rich flavour and dark colour.

It can be enjoyed hot or cold and is often served with milk, sugar, lemon or other flavours.

Black tea contains caffeine, which can help increase alertness and concentration. It also contains natural compounds called antioxidants.

Popular Varieties

  • English Breakfast
  • Earl Grey
  • Assam
  • Darjeeling
Brewing tip: Use freshly boiled water and steep black tea for 3–5 minutes to develop its rich flavour.
